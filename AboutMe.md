## 1. 🏛️ VikasDrishti – Hyper-Local Governance Platform

### National-Level Hackathon Project

**Live Demo:** https://vikasdrishti.netlify.app/
**GitHub:** https://github.com/lokeshm2845/VikasDrishti

VikasDrishti is a **hyper-local governance platform** designed to bridge the gap between citizens and government leaders through geospatial technology, AI, and transparent grievance management.

The platform focuses on delivering **location-specific development information and accountability** directly to citizens.

### ✨ Key Features

* 📍 Gali-level geofencing with street-level precision
* 📸 Photo-based proof of development
* 🔄 Before/after development verification
* 🌐 Multilingual communication
* 🤖 AI-powered translation
* 📢 Two-way citizen feedback
* 🚨 Automatic complaint escalation
* 📡 Offline-capable architecture
* 🗺️ Geospatial visualization

### 🛠️ Technology

**React.js • Supabase • PostgreSQL • PostGIS • Bhashini • Twilio • Firebase • OpenStreetMap**

### 🏆 Recognition

* 🥇 Top 15 Finalists – India Innovates 2026

* 🏅 Part of a Guinness World Record hackathon attempt

### 📊 Reported Impact

* **77% faster resolution**
* **14 days → 3.2 days** average resolution
